# Category: Apparel

Inspect color, neckline, shoulder seam, sleeve shape, waist cut, garment length,
and fabric texture/drape. Compare visible face/hair/body identity between generated
scene frames only; product photos do not prescribe the creator's identity.
Fit must follow the selected SKU/size and observed body relation. Do not infer
measurements, stretch, hidden seams, or rear graphics from a front-only photo.

One 2-row × 2-column reference sheet, 1024x1024: full front silhouette,
supported angled/rear view, fabric/seam detail, and verified worn fit/placement.
If an angle or fit has no evidence, repeat a supported view instead of inventing it.
Use only confirmed dressing/adjusting/display actions in their original order.
Keep skin, hands, cloth contact, gravity, seams and fabric behavior plausible.
