# Trait overlay: inflatable

Use for air beds, sleeping mats, pool products, inflatable toys, cushions and other products whose geometry depends on internal air pressure.

- Preserve chamber layout, seams, valve and pump inventory, material, inflated silhouette and source-backed dimensions.
- Keep inflated, partially inflated and deflated states distinct. Never depict melting, spontaneous growth, vanishing material or a change of SKU.
- Show a pump, hose, cable or removable module only when the exact component and connection are evidenced.
- Endpoint photos do not prove the inflation transition. Use an editor hard cut unless direct motion or an instruction diagram supports the intermediate states.
- Loaded products must keep plausible ground/support contact, deformation and scale without impossible collapse or floating.
