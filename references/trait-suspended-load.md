# Trait overlay: suspended load

Use for products whose function depends on ropes, straps, chains, cables or other suspension members, including hammocks, hanging chairs, swings, suspended storage and hanging equipment.

- Preserve the exact anchor count, suspension members, connection points and continuous load path supported by the source.
- Show anchors at physically compatible positions and keep every loaded suspension line under plausible tension.
- Reject floating products, one-ended loaded suspension, disappearing ropes, phantom stands or hardware, and attachments that pass through matter.
- Installation motion requires direct motion or instruction-diagram evidence. Endpoint photos support separate states and an editor hard cut only.
- When a person or payload is present, preserve source-backed capacity, contact, sag and relative scale.
