# Universal production-family checks

Apply these checks to every physical product. The production family selects a reusable reference-sheet layout; it does not replace SKU evidence.

- Preserve the complete canonical silhouette, visible part inventory, material, colour, proportions and defining interfaces.
- Show only source-backed views, functions, contacts and states. Repeat a supported view when a hidden side is unknown.
- Keep gravity, support surfaces, load paths, actor contact and relative scale physically coherent.
- Treat store taxonomy and production classification separately: the first says what the item is; the second says what visual evidence and interactions the video must control.
- Apply every physical-trait overlay and interaction constraint recorded in `production_classification`.
- Reject invented parts, controls, connectors, accessories, labels, mechanisms, states or uses.
- Use `general-merchandise` when no specialized production family fits, and require manual review when evidence is insufficient.
