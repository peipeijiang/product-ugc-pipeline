# Product UGC Pipeline

This skill turns ecommerce product pages into product-faithful short-form UGC ads. It performs source-complete extraction, visual analysis, product cognition, identity locking, low-risk creative planning, chronological storyboard generation, Omni Flash `omni-reference` video generation and QC.

Production videos always use LK888/upDrama `omni-flash` or `omni_flash-10s` with the reference order: storyboard, identity grid, optional operation grid. No other video route is part of the skill.
